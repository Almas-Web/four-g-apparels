About Us

A sourcing partner that works like your own team.

Four-G Apparels Limited is a professionally managed garments buying and sourcing company based in Dhaka. We connect international buyers with suitable production facilities that meet their requirements for quality, price, compliance, capacity and timely delivery.

Our team works across merchandising, sourcing, procurement, production coordination, quality assurance and logistics, so you get one well managed experience from product development to final shipment.
